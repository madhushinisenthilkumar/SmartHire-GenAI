# Switching to a Data Science Role

## Core skills needed
- Python (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn) is the dominant language for data science roles.
- Statistics and probability — hypothesis testing, regression, distributions.
- SQL for pulling and shaping data before modeling.
- Communicating results to non-technical stakeholders is as important as the modeling itself.

## Typical path for a career switcher
1. Build a strong foundation in statistics before jumping to advanced machine learning.
2. Complete 2-3 end-to-end projects: data cleaning, modeling, and a clear written summary of findings — not just a notebook of code.
3. Learn to explain a model's results in plain language; this is heavily tested in interviews.
4. Consider "Data Analyst" as a stepping-stone role if fully entry-level, since many Data Scientist postings expect 1-2+ years experience.

## Common mistakes
- Focusing only on the newest/most complex models instead of demonstrating solid fundamentals (linear regression, classification basics).
- Portfolio projects with no discussion of trade-offs, limitations, or why a particular model was chosen.

## Interview focus areas
- Statistics fundamentals (often asked conceptually, not just computationally)
- Case studies: "how would you approach predicting X for this business"
- Explaining a past project's methodology and results clearly
